Privacy Policy — Dangle

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Dangle (“the app”) is a puzzle game for Android, published by Shannon Studios (“we”). This policy explains what data is processed when you use it.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data ourselves. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your progress (solved levels, stars, streaks, hints, settings and the state of the level you are playing) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited, non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

More information:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

In-app purchase

“Remove Ads” is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether you own the purchase. See the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or share any data. Data processed by AdMob and Google Play is governed by Google’s policies linked above.
