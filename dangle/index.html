Dangle

Hang the weights, balance the mobile.

Drag the numbered weights onto the hooks until every rod hangs level. On each rod, weight × distance on the left must equal weight × distance on the right — and a lower rod weighs as much as everything hanging from it.

  • 500 levels in 10 worlds, from a cosy Nursery to a ticking Clockwork
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution
  • No timers, no account, works offline
  • English, Nederlands, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Português
