Atom Forge

Build every element, from hydrogen to oganesson.

Pull back, aim and shoot protons, neutrons and electrons to build a real atom, one particle at a time — then keep adding to the same atom until you have built all 118 elements. Neutrons fly straight, electrons curve in, protons are pushed away, and the nucleus sways as the atoms get heavier.

  • All 118 elements, hydrogen to oganesson, on one growing atom
  • Physics you can feel: aim, power, bank shots off the walls
  • Drifters, contaminated particles, unstable nuclei and radioactive decay
  • The view zooms out as new electron shells open
  • An info page for every element: protons, neutrons, stability, discovery
  • Upgrades after each element, a daily boost, and endless harder loops
  • No account, plays offline (ads are optional and only shown when you choose to watch one)
