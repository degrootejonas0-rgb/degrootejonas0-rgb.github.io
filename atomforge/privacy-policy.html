Privacy Policy — Atom Forge

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Atom Forge (“the app”) is a game for Android in which you build atoms, published by Shannon Studios (“we”). This policy explains what data is processed when you use it.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data ourselves. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your progress (elements you have built, scores, your best element, settings, and the day you last used the daily boost) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset Progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. An ad is only shown when you choose it, for example to start from a higher atom (“daily boost”) or to continue after a game over. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited, non-personalised ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or share any data. Data processed by AdMob is governed by Google’s policies linked above.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google, use the controls above or contact Google.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
